Klavitopia Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · Draft pending legal review.

Klavitopia is an app that helps children learn piano by playing. It is designed for minors, so we have done our best not to collect anything from them. This policy explains it in plain language.

In short

  • Everything stays on your device. Profiles, progress, stars, and songs are stored only on the phone or tablet. We have no servers where they could be stored.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no email address to provide.
  • No ads and no analytics. We do not show advertising or measure what the child does with third-party tools.
  • Only one connection: the purchase. If an adult buys the full version, the store (Apple or Google) and our purchase service (RevenueCat) process the purchase. This is the only thing that leaves the device.
  • No microphone, camera, location, or contacts. The app does not ask for them.

Who is responsible

What data is stored on the device

This data is not sent to anyone and you can delete it whenever you want:

DataPurpose
Profile name or nickname, age range, and avatarTo show the profile and adjust the difficulty to the child's age. It can be a nickname; the real name is not needed.
Progress, stars, skills, tests, and practice calendarTo continue the course and show "My week" and the parents' area.
Melodies invented and songs recorded or imported by the familyTo use them in the concert and in lessons.
Settings (sound, voice, keyboard delay, calibrated middle C, name of the connected MIDI keyboard)To remember your setup.
Whether the full version has been purchasedSo the app works without a connection.

The app can be used without an internet connection.

Purchases (the only connection)

To sell the full version we use App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat, a service that confirms purchases. When an adult opens the purchase screen or taps "Restore purchase":

  • The store handles the payment. We do not see or store any payment data.
  • RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier created for the installation and the purchase status (whether there is a purchase and which product). It does not receive the name, age, progress, or any other information about the child.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking identifiers.

RevenueCat information: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Before showing the purchase screen, the app asks an adult to solve a math problem. Children cannot buy on their own.

Children

The app is aimed at children aged 3 to 12, together with their parents or guardians. We do not collect personal information from children, there are no public profiles, messages, or community, and we do not include advertising or external links that the child can open.

If you are a parent or guardian and want to delete what is stored, you can do it on the device itself (see below). Since we do not have your data, there is nothing on a server for you to ask us to delete.

Files you choose

If the family imports a music score (MusicXML), the file is read only once, on the device; it is not sent anywhere. Only the resulting song is stored. Songs of their own are the responsibility of whoever imports or records them.

Device permissions

The app does not ask for access to the microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photos. It may use a USB or Bluetooth connection for a musical keyboard if you connect one, and the system voice to read the slides aloud (this is done on the device itself).

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Data is kept as long as the app is installed. You can:

  • delete a profile from within the app ("Who's playing?" → edit → delete),
  • use "Erase progress" in the settings (it restarts the course of the active profile),
  • use "Delete all data" in the settings (it asks a question for adults and leaves the app as if just installed), or
  • uninstall the app, which removes all data from the device.

Security

Because the data is only on your device, its security depends on the lock and backups you have set up on it. System backups (iCloud or Google) may include it if you have them turned on.

Your rights

Since we do not process your personal data on our systems, we have no data to access, correct, or delete. If you believe we do, or if you have any questions, write to us at bettodatatech@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es).

Changes

If we change something important (for example, if we added a feature that uses the microphone), we will update this policy and let you know in the app before the feature is used.
